# 10-second travel agency ad

Format: vertical 1080x1920, 10 seconds, cinematic.

## Storyboard
0.0–2.0s — Hero hook: realistic graphite Earth, sleek passenger airplane sweeping around it, warm coral travel route. On-screen: `Куда летим?`

2.0–5.0s — Rapid destination montage: tropical beach, Dubai skyline, Maldives lagoon. Premium cinematic cuts.

5.0–8.0s — Travel-agency reveal: route map and booking/trip-planning visual. On-screen: `Подберём. Забронируем. Организуем.`

8.0–10.0s — Final hero returns to globe + airplane. On-screen: `Твоё путешествие начинается здесь`.

## Direction
Dark graphite background, realistic photography/3D, elegant typography, warm coral-orange route accents, smooth cinematic motion, strong first-2-second hook, readable text, no visual clutter, no purple-blue gradients, no generic SaaS aesthetic.

## Brag invocation
`/brag --duration 10 --format vertical --tone cinematic --title "Travel Agency"`
