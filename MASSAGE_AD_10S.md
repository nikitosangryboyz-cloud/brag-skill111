- 1080x1920 vertical
- 10 seconds
- cinematic luxury wellness commercial
- premium massage / spa
- realistic photography
- smooth cinematic transitions
- large editorial Russian typography
- mobile massage booking app

0.0–2.0s — HERO HOOK
Extreme cinematic close-up of a massage therapist's hands slowly moving across a person's back.
Warm low-key spa lighting.
Large typography:
"МАССАЖ"
then:
"ДЛЯ ТЕЛА"

2.0–4.0s — RELAXATION
Continue the hand movement.

massage oil, skin, towels, warm spa interior.

"ВОССТАНОВИТЕ"
then:
"ТЕЛО"

4.0–6.0s — APP REVEAL

"ВЫБЕРИТЕ СВОЙ МАССАЖ"

"Классический"
"Расслабляющий"
"Спортивный"

6.0–8.0s — BOOKING
Camera smoothly pushes into the smartphone screen.
Show:
"ВЫБЕРИТЕ ДАТУ И ВРЕМЯ"

"ЗАПИСАТЬСЯ →"

8.0–10.0s — FINAL HERO
Return to cinematic massage footage.

"ПОЧУВСТВУЙТЕ
ЛЁГКОСТЬ"

"ЗАПИСАТЬСЯ →"

## Visual Direction

Warm cream, dark brown and black palette.
Natural skin tones.
Soft warm spa lighting.
Premium editorial photography.
Luxury wellness campaign aesthetic.
Large condensed typography.
Minimal interface.
Elegant handwritten accent typography.
Slow cinematic camera movement.
Smooth match cuts.
Object-based transitions.
Strong depth of field.
Realistic skin and hands.

## Motion

Slow camera push-ins.
Subtle handheld cinematic movement.
Smooth scale transitions.
Match cuts through hands and body movement.
Camera moves naturally between footage and smartphone UI.
No abrupt cuts.
No random motion.

## Typography

Large condensed uppercase Russian headlines.
Cream/off-white text.
Tight tracking.
Very large scale.
Typography is part of the composition, not standard UI.

## Avoid

No purple.
No blue gradients.
No neon.
No glassmorphism.
No generic SaaS aesthetic.
No cheap stock photography.
No generic spa clichés.
No excessive UI.
No random floating elements.
No glitch effects.
No TikTok-style transitions.
No oversaturated colors.
No people looking at camera.
No generic AI-looking imagery.
